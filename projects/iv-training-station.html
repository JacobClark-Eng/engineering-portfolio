Custom IV Training Station

Designed and manufactured a reusable training aid to hold an IV support pole and guide tubing for hands-on nursing education.

Project overview

I designed this station for a nurse educator at Hartford Hospital. The previous setup used a cup to support the pole and required someone to hold the tubing in place. My goal was to eliminate that extra assistance during training.

Measurement and design

I measured the pole circumference using paper and calculated its diameter (approximately 0.2586 inches). I modeled the base from scratch in Onshape with a 1.25-inch-deep socket and a longitudinal tubing channel.

Manufacturing and assembly

The 10.8-inch base exceeded the available printer build volume, so I divided it into 9-inch and 1.8-inch sections. I designed two side brackets and a T-shaped bottom bracket to reinforce the seam, printed them in PLA, and assembled the components using super glue. The side brackets measure 3 × 1 × ⅛ inches; the bottom bracket measures approximately 4 × 3.5 × ⅛ inches overall.

I learned Bambu Studio, printer operation, and makerspace procedures to manufacture the design independently.

Functional testing

The pole fits securely and can be removed and reinserted, the tubing stays in the channel, and the base remains stationary during normal setup and use. The completed station is awaiting its first trainee session at Hartford Hospital. Long-term joint durability has not yet been verified.
